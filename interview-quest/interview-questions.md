# 11. Questions interviewers may ask as follow-ups

Don't stop at the first answer. At 6–7 YOE, interviewers often drill down.

For example:

**Interviewer:** Why use Redux Toolkit?

**You:** It reduces Redux boilerplate and provides good defaults.

**Interviewer:** _What defaults?_

You should be ready to discuss:

- `configureStore`
- thunk middleware
- DevTools integration
- Immer
- serializable-state checking
- `createSlice`
- standardized patterns

Another example:

**Interviewer:** Why shouldn't we mutate Redux state?

**Follow-up:** _But RTK lets you mutate state. Why?_

**Follow-up:** _What exactly does Immer do?_

**Follow-up:** _Does Immer deep clone the entire state?_

These follow-up questions are where **6–7 YOE interviews often differentiate candidates**.
